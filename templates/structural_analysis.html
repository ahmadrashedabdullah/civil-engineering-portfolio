SAP2000 Seismic & Structural Analysis

Static & Dynamic Analysis of a Multi-Story Steel Building

This project evaluated the seismic behavior of a multi-story steel building using SAP2000 and supporting hand calculations. The analysis included structural modeling, seismic design parameters, building periods, base shear, story-force distribution, structural displacement, and static and dynamic seismic response.

SAP2000 Structural Analysis Seismic Analysis Structural Dynamics Response Spectrum IBC

01 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Objective

The objective of the project was to analyze the structural and seismic response of a multi-story steel building and compare the behavior of an original structural model with a revised model.

SAP2000 was used to model the structural system and perform static, modal, and dynamic analyses. Supporting hand calculations were completed to verify key seismic design parameters and structural response quantities.

The project combined computer-based structural modeling with hand calculations to evaluate and verify seismic behavior.

02 — STRUCTURAL MODEL

Multi-Story Steel Building

The structure was modeled as a multi-story steel building with different lateral-force-resisting systems in the two principal directions.

X Direction

Steel moment-frame behavior was evaluated in the X direction.

Y Direction

Steel braced-frame behavior was evaluated in the Y direction.

03 — STRUCTURAL DYNAMICS

Building Period of Vibration

Approximate fundamental periods were calculated using the building height and structural-system parameters. These values were then compared with the periods obtained from the structural model.

The comparison helped evaluate how changes to the structural configuration affected the dynamic behavior of the building.

04 — SEISMIC DESIGN PARAMETERS

Seismic Coefficients

Supporting calculations were completed to establish seismic design parameters used in the structural analysis, including site coefficients and design spectral response parameters.

SS

Short-period mapped spectral acceleration.

S1

One-second mapped spectral acceleration.

Fa & Fv

Site coefficients used to develop the design spectral response parameters.

SDS

Design spectral response acceleration at short periods.

SD1

Design spectral response acceleration at a one-second period.

Seismic Design Category

The supporting calculations classified the structure within Seismic Design Category D.

05 — BASE SHEAR

Seismic Base Shear

The seismic response coefficient was calculated for each direction and checked against applicable upper and lower limits.

Total seismic base shear was then calculated using:

V = CsW

The resulting base shear was used as the total lateral seismic force acting on the structure.

06 — STORY FORCE DISTRIBUTION

Vertical Distribution of Seismic Forces

After determining the total base shear, the seismic force was distributed vertically through the structure according to the seismic weight and elevation of each floor.

Story forces were calculated separately for the X and Y directions and compared between the original and revised structural models.

07 — STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS

Static & Dynamic Analysis

SAP2000 was used to evaluate the structural response under multiple analysis conditions.

Dead Load

Structural response under gravity loading.

Modal Analysis

Dynamic characteristics and vibration modes of the structure.

IBC Static

Equivalent lateral-force seismic analysis.

IBC Dynamic

Dynamic seismic response of the structural model.

08 — RESPONSE SPECTRUM

Response Spectrum Analysis

A response-spectrum function was defined in SAP2000 to evaluate the building response over a range of structural periods.

The analysis used a 5% damping ratio and provided a dynamic representation of the expected structural response under earthquake loading.

09 — STRUCTURAL RESPONSE

Displacement & Deformed Shape

Structural displacement and deformed shapes were reviewed in SAP2000 to understand how the building responded to lateral loading.

Results from the original and revised models were compared to identify differences in structural response.

10 — MODEL COMPARISON

Original vs. Revised Model

One of the main objectives was to compare the original structural configuration with a revised model.

Changes to the building configuration affected the dynamic properties, seismic-force distribution, and calculated structural response.

Original Model

Baseline structural configuration used for the initial seismic analysis.

Revised Model

Modified structural configuration used to evaluate the effects of changes to the building system.
