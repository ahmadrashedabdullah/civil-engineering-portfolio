CONSTRUCTION ESTIMATING • BIM • MIXED-USE DEVELOPMENT

Lawrence Plaza Mixed-Use Development

Model-Based Construction Cost Estimation | Santa Clara County, California

A construction estimating and cost analysis project involving the development of a large mixed-use property containing residential apartments, retail spaces, restaurant and hotel components, and an observation tower. The project integrated SketchUp, Revit, quantity takeoff, construction scheduling, and detailed cost estimating.

01 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

Lawrence Plaza Development

Lawrence Plaza Development is a conceptual mixed-use development located in Santa Clara County, California.

The proposed development includes residential apartment buildings with multiple unit configurations, senior living units, retail spaces, restaurant and hotel components, amenities, and an observation tower.

The project was developed as part of CE 232 — Construction Estimating & Cost Analysis and focused on connecting digital building models with quantity takeoff, scheduling, and construction cost estimating.

Digital Model → Quantity Takeoff → Schedule → Construction Cost Estimate

18 — ENGINEERING APPLICATION

BIM-Integrated Construction Estimating

This project demonstrated how digital building models can support construction estimating for a large, multi-component development.

Revit-based quantity extraction creates a direct connection between building design information and construction quantities, while estimating templates translate those quantities into project costs.

SketchUp also supported early visualization and site planning, while scheduling introduced the time component required for a more complete understanding of project execution.
